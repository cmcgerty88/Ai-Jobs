Your private workspace

Continue where you left off.

Create a workspace on this device. No email is needed. Your reviewed profile and job-search progress will be encrypted and saved here as you work.

This is device signup, with no online account or cross-device sync. Use a trusted personal browser. Your password cannot be reset: losing it means losing access. Saved data expires 30 days after the last save, and is removed when you next open the app. The workspace locks after 10 minutes without activity.

Use 14–256 characters, including at least one uppercase letter and one special character (for example !, @ or #). Don’t reuse a password from another service.

A shared or compromised device, browser extensions, or malicious site updates can still expose an unlocked workspace. Avoid full addresses, dates of birth, identity documents, health details and referees’ information.

Local library · no AI connection
Starting points, not a decision about your career.

These directions come from a limited library of business, service and technology-delivery roles. Your target role takes priority; otherwise we use your current role. They are not live vacancies or a qualification assessment.

How these directions are chosen

We put title connections first. Other suggestions must share a supported work area and responsibility level, with at least two confirmed skill connections including one linked to the role’s main work. Without a role title we use those skill rules alone. Titles never add skills or establish qualifications. If your occupation is outside the library, we say so instead of suggesting unrelated work. Browse the whole library if you deliberately want to explore a change of direction.

            Your information stays with you.
            
  
            Your CV is read in your browser. Files and the raw CV-text box are never saved by this app or uploaded. Only examples you choose to copy into your profile are retained. Avoid sensitive or unnecessary details.
            
  
            Creating a workspace encrypts your reviewed profile, preferences, role directions, vacancies, application outcomes, CV-builder details and drafts, interview practice notes and optional feedback in this browser. Changes save automatically while unlocked. The password and encryption key are not saved. Document generation is local and makes no external API calls. There is no server account, password reset, cloud sync or database. “Try without saving” keeps everything in this tab only.
            
  
            Use Lock workspace when finished. Ten minutes without activity also locks it and clears raw CV text. Encryption cannot protect an unlocked workspace from someone using your device, malicious extensions or compromised site code. Use a trusted device and a strong, unique password.
            
  
            Data expires 30 days after its last save. Expired data is removed on your next visit; this website cannot delete browser storage while it is closed. Clear my information deletes the app’s current inputs and both old and encrypted saved copies. Encrypted backups, downloaded CVs, interview packs and browser/device backups need separate deletion. CV and interview downloads are unencrypted documents you choose to create for use outside the app. Downloads can be restored on this landing page using your password before their expiry.
            
  
            GitHub Pages serves this site and may process normal hosting information, including your IP address, under GitHub’s privacy statement. No analytics, advertising, email or external AI calls are added. Opening a vacancy link visits that employer’s site. Optional pilot summaries contain counts and feedback only; review them before sharing directly with your pilot organiser. Do not send CVs or profiles through GitHub issues.
            
  
            Suggestions come from a limited editorial role library, your stated requirements and recorded outcomes. They do not establish eligibility, predict hiring or determine why an employer declined. This is a device-only pilot; hosted accounts and any future AI processing require a separate privacy and security review.
