Local library · no AI connection
Starting points, not a decision about your career.

These directions come from a small, manually written library of business, service and support roles. Connections use the skill labels you confirmed. They are not live vacancies, a qualification assessment, or evidence of salary or job availability.

How these directions are chosen

A direction appears when at least two of your confirmed skill labels match its library entry, including a skill linked to its main work. Up to five are shown, ordered by the number of connections. Your name, job title and raw CV are not used to guess skills. The library does not cover every career or wording; browsing it is always available.

        Your information stays with you.

        Your CV is read inside your browser. This version does not upload your file, send its text to an AI provider, create an account, or send email.

        Your profile, saved role directions and vacancies stay in this tab unless you choose Save on this device. That stores the reviewed profile, selected role directions and vacancy entries in this browser’s local storage. It does not store the original CV file or the CV-text box. Anyone with access to your browser profile may be able to see saved data; it is not a password-protected account.

        Clear my information removes the app’s saved data and current inputs. Downloaded files and backups made by your browser/device are outside the app and need separate deletion.

        The site loads its own code and CV readers from GitHub Pages. GitHub may process normal website request information, such as IP addresses, under its hosting policies. The app adds no analytics, advertising or third-party scripts. Opening an employer link takes you to that employer’s site.

        This is a personal exploration and comparison tool. Role directions come from a small editorial library using skill labels you confirm. It does not assess the meaning of your CV, infer qualifications or establish demand for a role. Vacancy comparisons check the facts you enter and literal skill mentions. It does not yet use AI, search for jobs, verify your eligibility, predict interview chances or provide legal/benefits advice. No personal information should be put into a GitHub issue or repository.

        Save on this device?

        This saves your experience profile, selected role directions and vacancy entries in this browser, replacing any previously saved profile. It does not save the original CV or its extracted text.

        Use this on a device and browser profile you trust. The saved data is not password-protected and does not follow you to another device.

        Clear your information?

        This clears your current CV text, profile, preferences, role directions and vacancies, plus any saved copy in this browser. It cannot remove files you downloaded or copies outside this app.

        Remove this vacancy?

        A previously saved copy on this device changes only when you save again.
